## 🎯 **Key Objectives**

Based on market analysis showing **13% YoY growth** in Cape Town tourism, our plans target:

1. **Market Leadership**: Achieve 20% market share within 24 months
2. **Revenue Growth**: 300% increase over 24 months (R100M annual run rate)
3. **Customer Experience**: 95%+ satisfaction scores across all personas
4. **Operational Excellence**: 99%+ tour completion rate with proactive infrastructure management

## 👥 **Target Personas**

Our technical implementation focuses on 5 primary personas:

1. **Emma the Experience Seeker** (Gen Z/Millennial) - Mobile-first, social-driven
2. **David the Cultural Explorer** (Millennial/Gen X) - Education-focused, research-heavy
3. **Sarah the Family Organizer** (Gen X) - Safety-conscious, group coordination
4. **Michael the Luxury Traveler** (Gen X/Boomer) - Premium service, convenience-focused
5. **Lisa the Conscious Traveler** (Millennial) - Sustainability-focused, impact-driven
