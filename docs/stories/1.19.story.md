# Story 1.19: Payment Integration for Custom Privat Tour Planner

## Goal
Enable users to securely pay for their custom/private bookings directly through the platform, integrating with the existing payment system.

## Why
- Streamlines the booking process for users and admins.
- Ensures bookings are confirmed only after payment.
- Increases conversion and revenue by reducing friction.

## What
- Integrate payment flow into the custom privat tour planner.
- Allow users to review their itinerary and total price before payment.
- Process payment using the existing payment provider (e.g., Stripe, Paystack).
- Update booking status to "paid" after successful payment.
- Notify users and admins of payment confirmation.

### Success Criteria
- [ ] Users can pay for custom/private bookings online.
- [ ] Payment is required before booking is confirmed.
- [ ] Booking status updates to "paid" after successful payment.
- [ ] Users and admins receive payment confirmation notifications.
- [ ] Payment failures are handled gracefully with user feedback.
