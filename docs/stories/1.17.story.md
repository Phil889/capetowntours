# Story 1.17: User Itinerary Editing & Booking Confirmation for Custom Privat Tour Planner

## Goal
Enable users to edit or cancel their custom/private itineraries after submission, and receive a confirmation email upon successful booking.

## Why
- Improves user experience and flexibility for custom/private bookings.
- Reduces admin workload by allowing users to self-manage their itineraries.
- Provides clear communication and confirmation to users.

## What
- Allow users to view, edit, or cancel their submitted custom/private itineraries.
- Send a confirmation email to the user after successful booking.
- Add validation for conflicting dates or unavailable tours in the itinerary.
- Update the admin dashboard to reflect user-initiated changes.

### Success Criteria
- [ ] Users can view and edit their custom/private itineraries after submission.
- [ ] Users can cancel their custom/private bookings.
- [ ] Users receive a confirmation email after booking.
- [ ] The system validates for conflicting dates or unavailable tours.
- [ ] Admins see updated status and changes in the dashboard.
