# Story 1.13: Guest Email Collection & Booking Confirmation Email

## Goal
Collect guest contact information during booking and send a confirmation email after successful payment.

## Why
- Enables communication with guests about their booking.
- Provides guests with a record of their reservation.
- Supports customer service and operational needs.

## What
- Add email (and optionally name/phone) fields to the booking widget.
- Store guest contact info with the booking in the database.
- Send a booking confirmation email to the guest after payment.
- Display a confirmation message on the booking confirmation page.
- Add error handling for email delivery failures.

### Success Criteria
- [ ] Guests must provide a valid email address during booking.
- [ ] Guest contact info is stored with the booking.
- [ ] Guests receive a confirmation email after payment.
- [ ] Confirmation message is displayed on the confirmation page.
- [ ] Email errors are handled gracefully.

**Status:** Implemented and validated. Guest email collection and confirmation email are live.
